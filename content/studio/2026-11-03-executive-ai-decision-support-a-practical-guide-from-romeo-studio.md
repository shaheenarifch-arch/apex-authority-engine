# Executive AI decision support: A Practical Guide from ROMEO Studio

## Executive summary

A practical overview of Executive AI decision support and how leaders can evaluate its role in modern operations.

## The business problem

Organizations need clearer ways to connect AI initiatives with measurable operating goals. Executive AI decision support should be evaluated against reliability, governance, cost, and business value.

## How the approach works

ROMEO Studio frames this topic around AI product creation, websites, apps, organic growth. The recommended approach is to begin with a controlled workflow, define approvals, maintain audit records, and measure outcomes before expanding automation.

## Practical use cases

- Reduce repetitive operating work.
- Improve access to structured business intelligence.
- Standardize repeatable workflows.
- Create reusable knowledge assets.

## Implementation considerations

Start in draft or approval mode. Protect credentials, validate source data, log changes, and avoid unsupported claims.
